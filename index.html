Our story

Little Munchkin Treats

This is Mom’s dessert shop — cake pops first, then the chocolate extras people keep asking for. Every pop is rolled, dipped, and decorated by hand so the tray looks like the logo: sweet, a little extra, and smiling.

We bake for birthdays, classroom parties, gender reveals, graduations, and thank-you boxes. If you found us on Instagram or TikTok, you are in the right place.

Ordering notes

Cottage-style bakery. Fresh, not factory.

How do I pay?

Place the order here. We confirm availability, then send Zelle, Venmo, or Cash App details. Card checkout can be added later with Square or Stripe.

Pickup or delivery?

Pickup is in the Fontana / Inland Empire area. Local drop-off can be arranged for a small fee depending on distance and tray size.

How far ahead should I order?

Standard boxes: 48–72 hours. Custom themed pops: 3–7 days. Holiday weeks fill fast.

Do you have allergens?

Treats are made in a home kitchen that may handle wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, and traces of nuts. Tell us about allergies in the notes.

Can I change colors?

Yes. Add a note at checkout or use the custom form for full theme matching.
